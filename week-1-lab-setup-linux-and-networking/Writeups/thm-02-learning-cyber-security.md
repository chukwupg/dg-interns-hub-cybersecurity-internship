## Lessons Learned
- Web application security focuses on identifying and mitigating vulnerabilities that could expose applications and their users to attacks.
- Network security involves protecting network infrastructure, communications, and connected systems against unauthorised access and other threats.
- Understanding different cybersecurity domains helps in identifying areas of interest and choosing a suitable career path.
- A structured learning roadmap helps organise learning, establish priorities, and build skills progressively.
- Practical labs and hands-on exercises are essential for reinforcing cybersecurity concepts and developing technical competence.
